# All Time Stats

The **All Time** view is the default landing page after uploading your files. It aggregates every single transaction to show your complete financial picture.

## Key Metrics

### Total Income & Expense
The absolute sum of all money coming in (credits) versus money going out (debits). This gives you the scale of your financial activity over the entire period.

### Net Result
The difference between **Total Income** and **Total Expense**.
* **Positive (Green)**: You earned more than you spent.
* **Negative (Red)**: You spent more than you earned.

### Category Breakdown
A visual Doughnut Chart showing where your money went.
* **Interactive**: Hover over (or tap) a slice to see the exact amount and percentage.
* **Click to Drill Down**: Clicking a category reveals a list of specific merchants/transactions within that category.

## Usage Tips
Use this view to answer big-picture questions:
* "What is my biggest expense category overall?"
* "Have I been net positive over the last few years?"
* "How much has my total income been since I opened these accounts?"
