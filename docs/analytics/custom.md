# Custom Analysis

Need to check a specific event, like a vacation or a project? Use the **Custom** tab.

## Features

The **Custom** tab gives you full control over the date range.

1. **Select Range**: Click "Select Date Range" to open the picker.
2. **Start & End Dates**: Choose any start date and end date. The dashboard will instantly recalculate everything—charts, totals, and lists—just for that specific period.
3. **Analyze**: Use this filtered view to answer questions like:
    * "How much did my trip to Batumi cost?"
    * "What was my total spending on 'Groceries' during my renovation?"

## Resetting
To clear the custom range, simply switch back to another tab (e.g., **All Time** or **Month**). Your custom selection is temporary and specific to this view.
