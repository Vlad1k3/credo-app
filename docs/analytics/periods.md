# Time Periods

Credo Analytics allows you to break down your data into manageable chunks for better comparisons.

## Available Perspectives

### Year View
View your finances month-by-month for a specific year.
* **Why use it?** Identify seasonal trends (e.g., higher spending in December).
* **Navigation**: Click the `<` and `>` arrows next to the year label (e.g., "2024") to switch between years.

### Month View
A detailed breakdown of a single month.
* **Why use it?** Check if you stayed within your budget for the month.
* **Navigation**: Jump between months (e.g., "January", "February") or click the date picker to select a specific month.

### Week View
Analyze your spending week-by-week.
* **Why use it?** Spot weekly habits, like weekend spending spikes.
* **Navigation**: Move between weeks (e.g., "Week 4") or select a week from the calendar.

### Day View
Zoom into a single day's transactions.
* **Why use it?** Audit specific days or trace individual transactions.

## Navigation Controls

When you select a period (other than All Time), a navigation bar appears:
* **Left Arrow (←)**: Go to the previous period (e.g., Previous Month).
* **Center Label**: Displays the current period. Click to open a **Date Picker** for quick selection.
* **Right Arrow (→)**: Go to the next period (e.g., Next Month).
