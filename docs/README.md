# Introduction

Welcome to **Credo Analytics**, a tool built to bring clarity to your financial chaos.

## Why this tool exists

As a developer and a user of Credo Bank, I found myself frustrated by the limitations of the official banking interface. It felt "wooden"—static, hard to navigate, and lacking in meaningful insights. I wanted to know more than just my current balance; I wanted to understand my spending habits, track my net worth over time, and visualize where my money was actually going.

Most existing solutions required giving up privacy—uploading bank statements to third-party servers or linking accounts via APIs that I didn't fully trust.

**Credo Analytics was born from this necessity.**

## What it does

This tool transforms the "uncomfortable numbers" hidden in your bank exports into a beautiful, modern, and interactive dashboard. It allows you to:

* **Visualize** your income and expenses with intuitive charts.
* **Filter** transactions by date, category, or amount.
* **Compare** spending across different time periods (Year, Month, Week, Day).
* **Analyze** specific events like vacations or projects with custom ranges.

## Privacy First

The core philosophy of this project is privacy.
**Your financial data never leaves your device.**
All processing happens locally in your browser. You can even use this tool without an internet connection.
